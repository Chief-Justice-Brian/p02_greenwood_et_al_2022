\noindent\textbf{Table 3: Crisis Probabilities by Price and Debt Growth Quantiles}

\smallskip
\noindent\footnotesize
Panel A presents the empirical distribution of country-years across equity-price
terciles and business-debt-growth quintiles. Panel B reports the probability of a
crisis within one to four years for each cell and the difference from the median
cell (price tercile 2, debt quintile 3). Panels C and D repeat the analysis for
household-debt growth and house-price growth. Differences are percentage points.
P-values use Driscoll--Kraay standard errors with lags 0, 3, 5, and 6 for horizons
one through four. *, **, and *** denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\%
levels. Gray cells identify the R-Zone (highest price tercile and debt quintile).
This replication reports estimates from the reconstructed historical panel and 
may vary from the published papers values.
