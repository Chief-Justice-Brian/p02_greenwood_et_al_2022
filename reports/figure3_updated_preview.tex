\noindent\textbf{Updated Figure 3: Fraction of Countries in R-Zone through 2025}

\smallskip
\noindent\footnotesize
For each sector and year, the figure reports the percentage of countries with
both required predictors that are in the R-Zone. Business combines
business-debt growth with real equity-price growth; household combines
household-debt growth with real house-price growth. The R-Zone remains the
intersection of the highest historical debt-growth quintile and highest
historical price-growth tercile. Thresholds are frozen at the historical
replication values; they are not recalibrated using post-publication data.
